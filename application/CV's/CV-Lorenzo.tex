\noindent\textbf{Research achievements and impact}

\noindent Lorenzo Loyola's research combines structural-engineering practice with digital construction. In industry he led the development of Revit add-ins that automate the transfer of structural design data into BIM reinforcement models, and carried out accelerometer-based monitoring and post-earthquake field assessments. At Aarhus University he deployed a real-time CO$_2$-monitoring digital twin on an active construction site with three industry partners (two first-author papers), built a BIM-based digital twin coupled to a Gazebo/ROS~2 virtual environment for simulated autonomous UAV bridge inspection (MSc thesis, submitted to i3CE 2026), and currently develops an ontology, Neo4j knowledge graph and LLM interface for structural monitoring data, the foundation of WP1 and WP2 of this proposal. Training: Urban Digital Twin PhD Spring School, London (2026); NRIS course on distributed LLM fine-tuning on HPC (2026).
