The PhD candidate, Lorenzo Loyola, is already employed at Aarhus University and can start on 1~January~2027. Consumables (equipment, running expenses and travel) are kept within the DKK~100{,}000 limit of the call; XR equipment covers one lightweight VR or AR headset with accessories, depending on the on-site versus remote use case selected with the stakeholders.

Costs for LLM/VLM use (self-hosted hardware and cloud API access for frontier models during development and user studies) are estimated at DKK~60{,}000 and will be covered in kind by the Structural Engineering section of the Department of Civil and Architectural Engineering, Aarhus University. These costs are therefore not included in the amount requested from CEBE.

The research stay at ETH Zurich exceeds what the travel budget can cover and will be funded through external mobility grants, for which the candidate will apply in the first project year. Danish foundations routinely support PhD stays abroad (e.g., [Otto M{\o}nsted Fond, Augustinus Fonden, William Demant Fonden]), in addition to the mobility support of the AU Graduate School of Technical Sciences.
