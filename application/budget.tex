The PhD candidate, Lorenzo Loyola, is already employed at Aarhus University and can start on 1~January~2027. Consumables (equipment, running expenses and travel) are kept within the DKK~100{,}000 limit of the call; XR equipment covers one lightweight VR or AR headset with accessories, depending on the on-site versus remote use case selected with the stakeholders. Costs for cloud computing ARE associated with AI developments. Travel budget will cover short staff exchanges.

The research stay at ETH Zurich will be funded through external mobility grants, for which the candidate will apply in the first project year. Danish foundations routinely support PhD stays abroad (e.g., [Otto M{\o}nsted Fond, Augustinus Fonden, William Demant Fonden]), in addition to the mobility support of the AU Graduate School of Technical Sciences.
